This example dataset is derived from the publicly available human colon organoid RNA-Seq study by Rodrigues et al. (2022).

The dataset includes gene-level count data and corresponding sample metadata for vehicle-control and doxorubicin-exposed human intestinal organoids.

For use in R-ODAF-Shiny, the data were processed according to the R-ODAF workflow and are provided here as a bundled example dataset for demonstrating the application.

Original study:
Rodrigues et al. (2022)
